## 🎯 **Game Rules Implemented**

1. **Game Start**: All cards shown briefly, then flipped face-down
2. **Gameplay**: Click cards to flip them face-up
3. **Matching**: When 2 cards are face-up, they're checked for matches
4. **Pairs**: Matching cards stay face-up and become unclickable
5. **Mismatches**: Non-matching cards flip back face-down after delay
6. **Victory**: Game ends when all pairs are found
7. **Scoring**: Based on moves, time, and difficulty level
