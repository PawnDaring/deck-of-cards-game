# Card Images Directory Structure

This directory should contain all the playing card images organized by suit.

## Expected Structure:

### Spades (♠) - Black suit
- A.png (Ace of Spades)
- 2.png through 10.png (Number cards)
- J.png (Jack of Spades)
- Q.png (Queen of Spades)  
- K.png (King of Spades)

### Hearts (♥) - Red suit  
- A.png (Ace of Hearts)
- 2.png through 10.png (Number cards)
- J.png (Jack of Hearts)
- Q.png (Queen of Hearts)
- K.png (King of Hearts)

### Diamonds (♦) - Red suit
- A.png (Ace of Diamonds)
- 2.png through 10.png (Number cards)  
- J.png (Jack of Diamonds)
- Q.png (Queen of Diamonds)
- K.png (King of Diamonds)

### Clubs (♣) - Black suit
- A.png (Ace of Clubs)
- 2.png through 10.png (Number cards)
- J.png (Jack of Clubs)
- Q.png (Queen of Clubs)
- K.png (King of Clubs)

## Image Specifications:
- **Format**: PNG with transparency preferred (JPG acceptable)
- **Size**: Consistent aspect ratio (recommended 2:3 ratio)
- **Recommended dimensions**: 140x200 pixels
- **Background**: Transparent or white
- **Naming**: Use the rank as filename (A.png, 2.png, etc.)

## Card Back Images:
Place card back designs in the `back/` folder:
- default.png (default blue back)
- classic.png (classic red back)  
- green.png (green pattern back)
- custom.png (custom design)

## Notes:
- All images should have the same dimensions for consistency
- Use high-quality images that scale well
- Consider providing 2x resolution versions for high-DPI displays
- Card designs should be clearly readable at small sizes
